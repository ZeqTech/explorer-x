# Code of Conduct

We want Explorer X to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background or experience level.

## Our standards

Expected behaviour:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent; ask questions before assuming mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Trolling or other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits and issues that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the email address on their GitHub profile or by opening a private security advisory on the repository. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
